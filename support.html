Common questions

How do I add my roster?
Three ways: import from your calendar, snap a photo of your roster, or tap shifts in. If your roster repeats (FIFO swings, ward rotations), tap “My roster repeats” and set it up once.
Why didn’t my alarm go off?
Check the alarm card on the night screen shows a tick. Kip needs permission to set alarms. If you said no, turn it on in Settings → Kip. Backup alarms add extra safety.
How do I cancel Kip Pro or the free trial?
Open Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Kip, then Cancel. Apple handles all billing; Kip reminds you a day before a trial ends.
How do I restore my purchase on a new phone?
Open the Kip Pro screen and tap “Restore purchases”, signed in with the same Apple ID.
Where is my data?
On your phone. Kip has no account or login. Profile → Erase my data removes everything. See the privacy policy.
Is Kip medical advice?
No. Kip is a wellness tool. It doesn’t diagnose, treat, or tell you whether you’re fit for work. Talk to a doctor about ongoing sleep problems.
